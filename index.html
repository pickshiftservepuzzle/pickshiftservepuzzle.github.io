Match dishes. Beat the clock.

Open bamboo baskets and match dishes on a moving conveyor in this kitchen puzzle game.

View on Google Play ↗

How to play

  1. Pick a basket

    Tap a bamboo basket to reveal the dish inside and send it onto the conveyor.

  2. Shift the belt

    Each new dish shifts the conveyor by one spot. When the oldest dish drops off, its basket closes again.

  3. Serve a match

    Fill the belt with identical dishes to serve a set. Complete every goal to clear the level.

Inside the game

50 levels
Work through different basket layouts and order goals.
Orders on the clock
Match the dishes shown at the top of the screen before the timer runs out.
Different belt sizes
Adjust your picks as levels change how many dishes fit on the belt.
