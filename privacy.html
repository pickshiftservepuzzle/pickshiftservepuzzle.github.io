Information and how it is used

Game progress and settings

The game saves your current level, attempt count, music, sound and vibration preferences, and whether you have completed the first-run setup on your device. It uses this information to restore your progress and preferences. Android may include this data in system backups or device transfers, depending on your settings. About Android Auto Backup

Advertising

Google AdMob provides optional rewarded video ads and interstitial ads. To deliver and measure ads and help prevent invalid traffic, Google’s advertising SDK automatically processes your IP address, which can indicate a general location, app and ad interactions such as taps and video views, and technical diagnostics such as launch performance and errors. It may process device or account identifiers when available, including the Android advertising ID. The SDK may also process the App Set ID for purposes such as analytics and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising partners may receive information for these advertising services. Google Mobile Ads data practices

Gameplay analytics

Google Analytics for Firebase helps us understand how the game is used and improve levels and gameplay. We send events such as game loading, opening the main menu, tapping Play, and starting or finishing a level. Events can include the level number, attempt number, outcome, and time spent. The SDK also processes information such as app launches, sessions, app and device details, an app instance ID, the Android advertising ID when available, and approximate location derived from IP. Google Analytics data practices

Crash reports and diagnostics

Firebase Crashlytics helps us find and fix crashes and other errors. Google receives crash and exception details, relevant app and device state, and installation identifiers. Firebase can also process device and app information and session activity to produce reliability metrics, and may include recent game events to help explain a crash. Privacy and security in Firebase

Game downloads and email

Google Play Asset Delivery uses device metadata and the app version to deliver the appropriate game files and keep them working after updates. Play Asset Delivery data practices

If you email us, Round Fox Games receives your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use it to answer your message and handle your request.

Advertising choices

Depending on your settings and the advertising services used, Google and its ad partners may collect information about your activity over time and across different apps or websites to personalize ads. You can manage supported ad settings in Android, including resetting or deleting the advertising ID where available. Deleting the ID limits its use. Other information may still be processed to deliver ads, measure their performance, or prevent fraud. Android ad controls · How Google uses data from apps that use its services

Storage, retention and security

To delete local progress and settings, clear the game’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the game. Android can restore data from a system backup, depending on your settings. Data already sent to an online service follows that service’s retention rules.

Our Google Analytics settings are 2 months for event data and 14 months for user-level data. New activity resets the 14-month period for the associated user identifier. Standard aggregate reports follow separate rules. Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from its systems. Google retains advertising data under its own retention policies. Round Fox Games retains support and privacy correspondence for 90 days. Google Analytics retention · Google data retention

Google uses encrypted connections to transmit the advertising and Firebase SDK data described here. Service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Google Privacy Policy

Privacy requests

Use the email below to ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy choices available under applicable law. Tell us which data or activity your request concerns. Gameplay analytics and crash reports use technical identifiers, so we may be unable to match an email address to those records. We will explain what we can do based on the information we can identify.

Children’s privacy

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we received personal information from your child, please email us so we can review the request.
